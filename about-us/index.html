About Us

This handbook was created by East Yellowstone Collaborative with robust support from many in our community.

The East Yellowstone Collaborative supports the conservation and economic resilience of working lands across the Absaroka Front. We value the essential role private lands play in sustaining wildlife habitat and landscape stewardship. We envision a well-stewarded, ecologically intact landscape along Wyoming’s Absaroka Front that supports wildlife populations and ranching operations for future generations.

The East Yellowstone Collaborative is: Beyond Yellowstone Living Lab, Greater Yellowstone Coalition, LegacyWorks Group, Park County Open Lands, The Nature Conservancy, Trout Unlimited and Western Landowners Alliance. Working Lands Advisors: Hoodoo Ranch, JBar9 Ranch, and Pitchfork Ranch.
